HAU RESEARCH AGENDA

Holy Angel University’s research agenda reflects a strategic integration of its mission, vision, and four strategic themes with a strong commitment to global sustainability and social responsibility. These priorities aim to cultivate a research culture that advances academic excellence, ethical leadership, and community development, while aligning with the United Nations' Sustainable Development Goals. By focusing on critical areas such as education, sustainability, technology, governance, and Catholic values, the university ensures that its research efforts address both local and global challenges, fostering meaningful societal impact and positioning itself as a forward-thinking, values-driven institution.
