What each role can do

Admin

URO staff. Manages users and roles, reviews completed research, updates proposal status, and approves or denies view requests.

Authorized Uploader

URO staff or thesis instructors who upload approved completed research with Dublin Core metadata.

Faculty

Dual role: submits and tracks research proposals here, and browses the repository separately via Knimbus SSO.

General users

Students and the HAU community sign in through Knimbus SSO, so they aren’t managed here. They can search, view abstracts, and request full-document access.

New accounts created here receive their URO username and temporary password by email.
